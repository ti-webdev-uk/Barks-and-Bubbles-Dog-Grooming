Our Story

Grooming built on
trust & tail wags.

Barks & Bubbles was born from a simple belief: every dog — no matter the breed, age, or coat — deserves a grooming experience that feels safe, gentle, and genuinely enjoyable.

We're a small, independent grooming studio in the heart of Kettering. Our groomers are fully certified and passionate dog people who treat every client like family. We take our time, read each dog's temperament, and never rush a single appointment.

No assembly lines. No shortcuts. Just honest, quality grooming done with love.
